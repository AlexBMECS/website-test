The opportunity

A visit is a snapshot.
Recovery is a changing story.

How someone moves and returns to their daily routine may add context to what care teams see in the clinic. We are exploring how those patterns can help identify changes in recovery.

01 / OBSERVE

Everyday signals

Study activity and mobility data captured by phones and wearables during daily life.

02 / UNDERSTAND

A personal reference point

Model an individual’s patterns over time and investigate changes from their usual activity.

03 / EVALUATE

Clinically meaningful questions

Test whether those changes add useful information about readmission risk after discharge.
