VitalCue’s initial focus is readmission prediction in kidney transplant recipients. The aim is to understand whether everyday activity adds information beyond a clinical snapshot.

See related transplant research

Research question

What might changing activity tell us?

We want to study how an individual’s movement patterns evolve after discharge, and whether those changes are associated with a later hospital readmission.

Study protocols will define the observation period, outcomes, data sources, and evaluation methods.

Common questions

About the program

Is VitalCue available for routine clinical care?

The work described here is in research and development. This website does not provide a clinical prediction service or patient monitoring.

Can patients enroll through this website?

This website does not enroll participants. Any participation would be through an approved study with its own eligibility requirements and consent process.

Does the platform replace clinical follow-up?

No. The research aims to explore additional context between visits. Patients should continue the follow-up and communication plan provided by their transplant team.

Are results from other studies VitalCue performance results?

No. The research page describes independent work that informs our approach. It does not establish performance in a VitalCue study.
