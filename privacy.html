Website privacy

A simple informational website.

This notice describes this public website, not a research app or clinical study.

What this website collects

The website code does not use analytics, advertising trackers, cookies, or a patient data collection system. Fonts and site assets are served with the site, without third-party font requests.

Contact inquiries

If an email inquiry option is available, it prepares a draft in your email application. Your inquiry is sent only when you choose to send it there. The website does not store the form content.

Hosting and external links

The hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, under its own policies. Research links open external websites with separate privacy practices. For GitHub Pages hosting, see the GitHub privacy statement.

Research participation

This website does not enroll participants or collect wearable measurements. Any research participation requires a separate consent process and study-specific privacy information.

Medical information

The content is informational and does not provide medical advice or real-time monitoring. Do not submit patient identifiers or medical records through general contact channels.
