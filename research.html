Peer-reviewed · 2024

Wearable data & readmissions

Impact of wearable device data and multi-scale entropy analysis on improving hospital readmission prediction

Nagarajan et al. · Journal of the American Medical Informatics Association

A retrospective study of 612 participants from All of Us examined clinical data, wearable time series, and multiscale entropy for unplanned readmission prediction up to 90 days after discharge.

Why it matters: patterns within activity data may add information beyond summary measures. The authors call for prospective validation.

Peer-reviewed · 2025

Kidney transplantation

Development of a machine learning-based prediction model for early hospital readmission after kidney transplantation: a retrospective study

Chong & Yeom · Journal of Korean Academy of Nursing

A retrospective study of 470 kidney transplant recipients evaluated machine learning models for readmission within 30 days.

Why it matters: this provides clinical context for the transplant readmission problem. It is not a wearable-based VitalCue validation study.

Wearable feature engineering

Prediction of Unplanned Hospital Readmission using Clinical and Longitudinal Wearable Sensor Features

Yhdego et al. · medRxiv

Explores clinical variables and longitudinal wearable sensor features, including multiscale entropy, for 90-day readmission prediction.

Research context: an early preprint in this area, not an independent confirmation of VitalCue’s performance.

Preprint · 2026

Dynamic prediction

Dynamic Prediction for Hospital Readmission in Patients with Chronic Heart Failure

Farina et al. · arXiv

Investigates a Bayesian joint model linking longitudinal NT-proBNP measurements to rehospitalization or death in chronic heart failure.

Methodological context: illustrates updating risk using repeated measurements. The population and biomarker differ from VitalCue’s initial focus.

Zhang et al. · Applied Intelligence
