About VitalCue

Understanding health
through everyday life.

VitalCue brings a clinical question to a computational challenge: what can everyday patterns tell us about how a person is doing?

Our origins

From mobility sensing
to continuity of care.

VitalCue grew from a project exploring smartphone sensors, kinetic data, and behavioral patterns in kidney disease. Early work focused on capturing movement data and investigating how changes in routine might reflect changes in health.

Our current direction starts with wearable data and hospital readmission prediction in kidney transplant recipients, with a longer-term goal of studying the approach in other populations.

The central idea remains the same: everyday life contains information that a single visit may not capture.
